    An Irish Songbook

    An Irish Songbook is a heartfelt celebration of Ireland's musical heritage, reimagined through the soulful lens of jazz.

    Curated by Simon Morgan and Richie Buckley's Quintet, a collective of Ireland's most accomplished musicians, this project unites some of the country's finest jazz artists to explore the timeless beauty of Irish melody in a fresh, contemporary way.

    Blending classical craft, jazz improvisation and traditional lyricism, An Irish Songbook offers sensitive new arrangements of beloved songs — from haunting airs to joyful ballads — each reshaped with warmth, depth and emotional sincerity.

    At its core, the project is about connection: between past and present, between genres and between musicians and audience. It invites listeners to hear Ireland's songs anew — familiar yet transformed, rooted in tradition yet alive with the spontaneity of jazz.
